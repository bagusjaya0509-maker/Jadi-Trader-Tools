Modifications: skeleton rig, digital expression morphs, sample animation clips,
typing animation, browser material adaptation, NPC behavior, navigation, camera
controls, and market displays created with OpenAI Codex.

Office: the interior reconstruction created earlier in this conversation from
the user's supplied reference. Existing furniture and architectural layout reused.
Market charts: public Hyperliquid prices and candles. Analyst performance and
active signal summaries: public JadiTrader feeds. Demo and delayed states are
identified in the interface. The decorative depth columns are illustrative.

Rendering: Three.js 0.180.0, MIT license (see vendor/LICENSE).

City window panorama: generated for the user as a flat architectural window texture,
updated to morning daylight, September 2026. No exterior building geometry.
The panorama is projected onto flat glazing with distant-view parallax,
atmospheric haze and angle-dependent room reflections.

Morning lighting update: directional window sunlight, stable ceiling shadow
occlusion, recessed area lighting, satin oak relief, room reflection probe,
contact occlusion and restrained light bloom. Three.js r180 official addons:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180/examples/jsm

Editable scene update: 159 individually named physical objects recovered from
the original source model. Plant parts are joined per plant; furnishings and
accessories are separate. Glass partitions, boundary clearance and navigation
were repaired. Manual low, balanced and high rendering profiles were added.

Office extension: supplied by the user as office-room.zip (FBX and chair textures).
Adaptation: unified oak floor, physical entrance, furniture scale matching, missing-material restoration, separated objects and optimized geometry.
Original office and robot attribution above remains applicable to those respective assets.

Trading desks, monitors, keyboards, dance routines and data adapter: selected
from the user's JadiTrader-TradingFloor-untuk-ChatGPT.zip. The uploaded duplicate
room is omitted; only the requested workstation assets and behavior are reused.
No music recordings were included in that upload.
